## What the app is

MediaForge is a **passive client**: a general-purpose graphical front end for the open-source tools `yt-dlp` and `FFmpeg`. Given a URL, it asks the source site for the media streams it already makes available and saves them to your computer. The app does not host, index, or distribute any media itself.
